From amount to money, step by step.

No branch, no queue. Here is exactly what happens after you choose an amount — including what happens if we can't lend to you.

  1. 1

    Choose your amount

    Pick R500 to R2 000 and slide to your next payday, 5 to 30 days away. The total you'll repay is shown straight away, and it can only go down after approval.

  2. 2

    Apply online

    Create your account, confirm your cellphone number on WhatsApp, then upload a copy of your ID, your latest payslip and your last three months of bank statements.

  3. 3

    A person reviews it

    Someone on our team checks your documents and whether the repayment fits your budget — the affordability test the National Credit Act requires.

  4. 4

    You get a decision

    You hear back either way. If we can't lend to you, we tell you and explain why. Applying costs nothing.

  5. 5

    Your money is paid out

    If you're approved, you authorise one DebiCheck debit order for your exact repayment. Then we pay the cash into your bank account.

Questions people ask.

Something else? Email hello@snapcash.money.

How quickly will I get the money?

We review applications as they come in. Once you're approved and have authorised your DebiCheck debit order, we pay the cash into your bank account. How long the review takes depends on how complete your documents are.

Am I guaranteed a loan if I apply?

No. We have to check that the repayment fits your budget before we can lend, as the National Credit Act requires. If we can't approve you, we'll tell you and explain why. Applying doesn't cost anything.

What does it cost?

Three charges, all capped by the National Credit Act: a once-off initiation fee, a service fee of R60 a month charged for your term, and interest of 0,17% a day. The calculator shows the exact total for your amount and term.

Which documents do I need?

A copy of your South African ID, your latest payslip and your last three months of bank statements. We never ask for your online banking password.

Why do you ask for my payday?

Your repayment is collected on your payday, so the money is in your account when the debit order runs. Your payday must be 5 to 30 days away. If yours is sooner than that, you can apply in time for your following payday.

Can I settle early?

Yes, at any time. You only pay interest for the days you had the money, and there's no early-settlement penalty.

Why do you send a WhatsApp code every time I log in?

So that someone who learns your password still can't get into your account or see your details. It takes a few seconds.
