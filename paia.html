PAIA Manual

Manual in terms of section 51 of the Promotion of Access to Information Act 2 of 2000 (PAIA) · Version 0.9 (pre-launch)

Template notice: This manual is a pre-launch draft. Company registration details, the Information Officer's particulars and physical addresses will be completed before live operations begin, and the final manual will be lodged as required.

1. Introduction

PAIA gives people the right to request access to records held by private bodies where the record is required for the exercise or protection of a right. This manual explains how to make such a request to Sequence Industries.

2. Contact details

Head of the private body: to be confirmed. Information Officer: to be appointed. Postal and physical address: to be confirmed, Johannesburg, South Africa. Requests may be directed in writing, and pending publication of dedicated details.

3. The section 10 guide

The Information Regulator has published a guide on how to use PAIA. It is available from the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg.org.za and from its offices.

4. Records we hold

  • Company records: statutory, governance and financial records.
  • Customer records: credit applications, credit agreements, affordability assessments, POPIA consent records, transaction and collection records (retained as required by the National Credit Act).
  • Operational records: supplier agreements, technology and security records, correspondence.
  • Personnel records: employment-related records, where applicable.

5. Records automatically available

The content of this website — including product terms, pricing, this manual and our privacy policy — is available without a PAIA request.

6. How to request a record

  1. Complete the prescribed request form (Form 2 of the PAIA Regulations, available from the Information Regulator).
  2. Submit it to our Information Officer with proof of identity, a description of the record, and the right you seek to exercise or protect.
  3. Pay the prescribed request fee where applicable. We respond within 30 days as PAIA requires, subject to the extensions and grounds of refusal set out in the Act (including protection of third-party privacy).

7. Remedies

If a request is refused, the requester may lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa) or apply to court, as provided for in PAIA.
