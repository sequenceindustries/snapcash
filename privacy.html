Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026 · Version 0.9 (pre-launch) · Issued under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA)

Template notice: This policy is a working pre-launch draft and will be finalised, together with the appointment and registration of our Information Officer, before live lending begins.

1. Who is responsible for your information

Sequence Industries is the "responsible party" under POPIA. Our Information Officer can be reached via the the National Credit Regulator (dedicated contact details to be published at launch).

2. What we collect

  • Identity: name, South African ID number, contact details.
  • Financial: employment status, income, bank account details, and — with your consent, via Stitch — your last 3 months of bank statements.
  • Credit: your credit bureau profile, with your consent.
  • Usage: basic technical logs when you use the website (for security and to make the site work).

3. Why we process it

We process personal information to: verify who you are; assess affordability as required by the National Credit Act; conclude and manage your credit agreement; disburse and collect payments; prevent fraud; meet legal obligations (including NCA record-keeping and reporting); and respond to your queries. We do not sell personal information, and we do not send marketing without a separate, optional consent.

4. Consent, recorded

Before we process anything for a credit application, we ask for your explicit consent — separately for personal-information processing, bank statement retrieval, credit bureau enquiries and DebiCheck mandate registration. Each consent is stored with its exact wording, version, timestamp and technical origin, so there is never doubt about what you agreed to.

5. Who we share it with

  • Stitch — secure bank linking and statement retrieval (we never see your banking password).
  • Payment partners — Ozow (payouts) and our DebiCheck sponsor bank (collections).
  • Registered credit bureaus — enquiries during assessment and reporting as required by the NCA.
  • Regulators and authorities — where the law requires it.
  • Service providers — hosting and infrastructure providers bound by confidentiality and POPIA-compliant agreements.

6. How long we keep it

We keep records for as long as the law requires — the NCA prescribes retention periods for credit agreements and related records — and no longer than necessary after that. Information tied to deleted or abandoned applications is removed on a defined schedule.

7. How we protect it

Data is encrypted in transit, access is restricted on a need-to-know basis enforced at the database level, and consent and transaction records are kept in tamper-evident, append-only form. No system is perfectly secure, but security is designed into ours rather than added on.

8. Your rights

Under POPIA you may: request access to the personal information we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate information; request deletion where we are not legally required to retain it; object to processing; and withdraw consent (which may mean we cannot continue with an application). To exercise any of these, use the the National Credit Regulator. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforeg.org.za.

9. Cookies

This website uses only what is necessary for it to function. We will publish a detailed cookie notice if that ever changes.

10. Changes

We will update this policy as the product evolves, and the version and date at the top will always tell you what you're reading.
