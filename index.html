Eligibility criteria

Check your loan eligibility.

Four things decide whether we can lend to you. If they all hold, you can apply right now — it costs nothing to find out.

Apply
01

Identity

A valid South African ID number, 18 or older. We verify you electronically when you link your bank — the account holder must match the applicant. No certified copies, no branch visits.

02

Income

Verifiable income over the last 3 months — salary, wages or regular self-employed earnings, read directly from your linked bank statements. What counts is what actually lands in your account.

03

Affordability

The National Credit Act requires the repayment to fit your real budget — income minus commitments and living costs. If the numbers say a loan would strain you, we decline. That's protection, not punishment.

04

Bank account

A South African bank account in your own name with internet banking — where we pay your money instantly, and where your single DebiCheck repayment comes from.

Frequently asked.

How fast will I get the money?

Once your application is approved and you've confirmed your DebiCheck mandate, we pay out through real-time clearing. For most banks the money reflects within minutes, any day of the week.

Do I need to send payslips or bank statements?

No. You link your bank account securely, which lets us verify your identity and read your last 3 months of statements automatically. You never upload a document and we never see your banking password.

What does it cost?

Three charges, all capped by the National Credit Act: a once-off initiation fee, a service fee of R60 per month pro-rated to your term, and interest of 0,17% per day. The calculator at the top shows your exact total before you apply.

What if I want to settle early?

You can settle at any time. Interest is only charged for the days you actually held the money, and there is no early-settlement penalty — so paying back sooner always costs you less.
