Frequently asked.

How fast will I get the money?

Once your application is approved, your documents confirmed and your debit order authority signed, we move quickly to pay your loan out to the bank account you gave us.

Do I need to send payslips or bank statements?

Yes — a copy of your ID, your latest payslip and your last 3 months' bank statements. Our team reviews these directly to confirm your identity and affordability. We never ask for your online banking password.

What does it cost?

Three charges, all capped by the National Credit Act: a once-off initiation fee, a service fee of R60 per month pro-rated to your term, and interest of 0,17% per day. The calculator at the top shows your exact total before you apply.

What if I want to settle early?

You can settle at any time. Interest is only charged for the days you actually held the money, and there is no early-settlement penalty — so paying back sooner always costs you less.

What happens if my application isn't approved?

You'll be notified with a short explanation, and there's no cost or record penalty for applying. You're welcome to reapply once your situation changes — many applicants who don't qualify the first time do later.

Is my personal information safe?

Yes. Your data is processed under POPIA, encrypted in transit, and only used to assess and administer your loan. We never sell your information, and every consent we rely on is recorded with a timestamp you can review.
